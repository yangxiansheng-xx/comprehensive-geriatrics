Submission Guidelines

Comprehensive Geriatrics accepts submissions through our online manuscript submission system. Before submitting, please ensure your manuscript complies with all guidelines outlined on this page.

Submission Checklist

  • Manuscript is within the journal's Aims & Scope
  • Article type and word count comply with our requirements (see below)
  • All required sections are included (Abstract, Keywords, Introduction, etc.)
  • References are formatted in Vancouver style
  • Figures and tables meet resolution and formatting requirements
  • All authors have approved the final manuscript
  • Required declarations are completed (ethics, conflicts of interest, funding)
  • Plagiarism check has been performed

Submission System: Manuscripts must be submitted through our online submission portal. We do not accept submissions via email. Access the submission system →

Article Types

Article Type Word Limit Abstract Description
Original Research ≤ 8,000 Structured, ≤ 250 words Reports of original clinical or translational research, including RCTs, cohort studies, case-control studies
Review Articles ≤ 10,000 Unstructured, ≤ 300 words Systematic reviews, narrative reviews, and meta-analyses. Protocol registration required for systematic reviews
Brief Reports ≤ 3,000 Unstructured, ≤ 150 words Concise reports of novel findings, pilot studies, or preliminary data
Case Reports ≤ 4,000 Unstructured, ≤ 200 words Unique or instructive cases with clear educational value. Informed consent required
Letters to the Editor ≤ 1,500 None Comments on published articles or brief observations
Editorials ≤ 2,500 None Invited only — Commentary on current topics by experts
Study Protocols ≤ 5,000 Structured, ≤ 250 words Detailed protocols for ongoing or planned clinical trials and studies

Formatting Requirements

General Formatting

  • Font: Times New Roman, 12pt
  • Spacing: Double-spaced throughout
  • Margins: 2.5 cm (1 inch) on all sides
  • Page numbers: Bottom center
  • Line numbering: Continuous, left margin

Manuscript Structure

  1. Title Page: Title, running head, author names and affiliations, corresponding author contact
  2. Abstract: As specified by article type (structured or unstructured)
  3. Keywords: 4-6 keywords for indexing
  4. Introduction: Background and study objectives
  5. Methods: Study design, setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, statistical analysis
  6. Results: Findings presented in logical sequence with tables/figures
  7. Discussion: Interpretation, implications, limitations, conclusions
  8. Declarations: Ethics, consent, conflicts, funding, data availability, author contributions
  9. References: Vancouver style, numbered consecutively

Figures & Tables

  • Resolution: Minimum 300 DPI at final print size
  • Formats: TIFF, EPS, or high-quality PNG preferred
  • Color: RGB color mode; ensure readability in grayscale
  • Figure legends: Provide as a separate document or at the end of the manuscript
  • Tables: Editable format (Word table or Excel), not as images
  • File size: Individual files should not exceed 10MB

References

References should be numbered consecutively in the order they appear in the text using Arabic numerals in square brackets [1, 2]. Use the Vancouver reference style. Examples:

  • Journal article: Smith J, Doe A. Title of article. Journal Name. Year;Vol(Issue):pages. doi:xxx
  • Book: Johnson R. Book Title. City: Publisher; Year.
  • Website: Organization Name. Title of page. URL (accessed Month Day, Year).

We strongly recommend using a reference manager (EndNote, Zotero, Mendeley) to ensure accuracy.

Ethics & Declarations

All manuscripts must include the following declarations in a dedicated section before the references:

Human Participants Research

  • Ethical approval: State the name of the ethics committee and approval number
  • Informed consent: Confirm written informed consent was obtained from all participants
  • Clinical trials: Must be registered in a WHO-approved registry (e.g., ClinicalTrials.gov). Include registration number and date in the abstract
  • De-identification: Ensure no identifying information is included in manuscripts

Animal Research

  • State the name of the ethics committee/institutional animal care committee
  • Follow the ARRIVE guidelines for reporting animal research

Required Declarations

  • Competing interests: All authors must disclose any financial or non-financial competing interests
  • Funding: State all sources of financial support, including grant numbers
  • Author contributions: Specify each author's contribution using CRediT taxonomy
  • Data availability: State where data can be accessed, or "available upon reasonable request"
  • Acknowledgments: Acknowledge non-author contributors with written permission

AI-Assisted Writing: If generative AI was used in manuscript preparation, this must be disclosed in the Methods or Acknowledgments section. AI tools cannot be listed as authors.

Copyright & Licensing

Upon acceptance, authors retain copyright of their work under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). This license allows others to:

  • Share — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format
  • Adapt — remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, even commercially

Under the following terms: Attribution — You must give appropriate credit, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.

Article Processing Charges (APC)

To support open access publication, Comprehensive Geriatrics charges an APC upon article acceptance. The APC covers the costs of editorial processing, peer review management, production, and online hosting.

Article Type APC (USD)
Original Research $1,500
Review Articles $1,500
Brief Reports $1,000
Case Reports $1,000
Letters to the Editor $500

APC Waivers & Discounts

  • Low-income countries: Full APC waiver for corresponding authors from countries classified by the World Bank as low-income economies (list available on request)
  • Lower-middle-income countries: 50% APC discount
  • Financial hardship: Case-by-case waivers may be granted upon written request with justification

To request a waiver, contact the Editorial Office before or at the time of submission.

Preprint Policy

Comprehensive Geriatrics permits the posting of manuscripts on preprint servers (e.g., bioRxiv, medRxiv, Research Square) prior to submission or during the peer review process.

  • Authors must disclose any preprint posting at the time of submission
  • If accepted, authors are encouraged to update the preprint with the final published version link
  • The published journal version is considered the Version of Record
