Aims & Scope

Comprehensive Geriatrics is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research that advances the understanding and care of older adults. The journal covers the full spectrum of geriatric medicine and gerontology, providing a comprehensive platform for researchers, clinicians, nurses, policymakers, and all professionals dedicated to improving the health and well-being of aging populations.

Our scope encompasses the following core areas:

Clinical Geriatrics

  • Age-related diseases and syndromes (dementia, frailty, sarcopenia, delirium)
  • Geriatric assessment and comprehensive care models
  • Fall prevention, mobility, and functional rehabilitation
  • Palliative and end-of-life care for older adults
  • Geriatric emergency medicine and acute care
  • Continuity of care and transitional care

Geriatric Biology & Translational Research

  • Cellular and molecular mechanisms of aging
  • Immunosenescence and inflammaging
  • Genetics of longevity and age-related diseases
  • Biomarkers of aging and biological clocks
  • Stem cell biology and regenerative medicine in aging

Social & Behavioral Gerontology

  • Cognitive aging and mental health in older adults
  • Social determinants of healthy aging
  • Long-term care, healthcare systems, and health policy
  • Age-friendly environments and age-friendly cities
  • Technology and digital health for aging populations

Geriatric Nursing & Allied Health

  • Nursing care models for older adults
  • Rehabilitation and physical therapy in geriatrics
  • Nutrition, metabolism, and dietary interventions
  • Geriatric pharmacology and polypharmacy management
  • Occupational therapy and quality of life

Target Audience: Researchers, clinicians, nurses, pharmacists, physical therapists, occupational therapists, social workers, policymakers, and all professionals dedicated to improving the health, independence, and quality of life of older adults worldwide.

Journal History

Comprehensive Geriatrics was officially launched in 2025 to address the growing need for an interdisciplinary, open-access platform in the field of aging research. Recognizing that the health challenges of aging populations span clinical medicine, biological science, social policy, and healthcare systems, our founders envisioned a journal that would truly live up to its name — comprehensive.

  • 2025 — Comprehensive Geriatrics officially launched (Volume 1, Issue 1)
  • 2025 — First articles published online
  • 2025 — Applied for indexing in PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, and DOAJ

Open Access Policy

All articles published in Comprehensive Geriatrics are made immediately available worldwide under an open access license. This means:

  • Free access — Readers have unrestricted, free access to all published content
  • Immediate availability — Articles are published online as soon as they are ready
  • Broad reuse — Content can be shared, adapted, and built upon with proper attribution
  • No embargo — There is no delay between publication and open access

Authors retain copyright of their work under a CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution) license. This allows anyone to use, distribute, and reproduce the work in any medium, provided the original author and source are properly credited.

Article Processing Charge (APC): To support open access publishing, authors are required to pay an APC upon acceptance. See our For Authors page for detailed fee information and waiver policies for authors from low-income countries.

Peer Review Policy

Comprehensive Geriatrics employs a double-blind peer review process. This means that both the reviewers and the authors remain anonymous to each other throughout the review process, ensuring impartial and unbiased evaluation.

Review Process

  1. Initial Check — The Editorial Office checks submissions for completeness, scope fit, and plagiarism screening.
  2. Editorial Assessment — The Editor-in-Chief or Associate Editor assesses whether the manuscript meets the journal's scope and quality standards.
  3. Peer Review — At least two independent experts in the relevant field review the manuscript. Reviewers evaluate: originality, methodology, data quality, clinical relevance, and clarity of presentation.
  4. Editorial Decision — Based on reviewer recommendations, the Editor makes one of the following decisions: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, or Reject.
  5. Revision (if applicable) — Authors revise the manuscript according to reviewer comments and resubmit.
  6. Final Decision — The Editor makes the final decision and notifies the authors.

Review Timeline: The median time from submission to first decision is approximately 21 days. Accepted articles are typically published online within 2 weeks of final acceptance.
