Call for Papers

Special Issue: Digital Health Interventions for Aging Populations

📅 Announced June 10, 2025 | Submission Deadline: December 31, 2025

We are inviting submissions for a special issue on digital health technologies designed to improve the health, independence, and quality of life of older adults. Topics include telemedicine, wearable devices, AI-assisted care, remote monitoring, and digital therapeutics.

Guest Editors: Prof. Sarah Mitchell (Stanford University), Prof. Wei Liu (Peking University)

Call for Papers

Special Issue: Multimorbidity and Integrated Care in Geriatric Medicine

📅 Announced May 28, 2025 | Submission Deadline: March 15, 2026

This special issue aims to advance understanding of multimorbidity patterns, disease interactions, and integrated care models for older adults with multiple chronic conditions.

Guest Editors: Prof. James Carter (UCL), Prof. Maria Santos (University of São Paulo)

Journal News

Comprehensive Geriatrics Now Indexed in Scopus

📅 June 1, 2025

We are pleased to announce that Comprehensive Geriatrics has been accepted for indexing in Scopus, the world's largest abstract and citation database of peer-reviewed literature. This milestone significantly enhances the visibility and discoverability of articles published in our journal.

Journal News

Applications Open for 2025 Early Career Researcher Editorial Fellowship

📅 May 15, 2025

Comprehensive Geriatrics is launching an Editorial Fellowship program for early career researchers (within 5 years of PhD or final training). Fellows will gain hands-on experience in editorial decision-making, peer review management, and journal operations. Applications close July 15, 2025.

Award

2025 Best Paper Award Winners Announced

📅 May 1, 2025

The Editorial Board is delighted to announce the winners of the 2025 Comprehensive Geriatrics Best Paper Awards, recognizing outstanding contributions published in our first volume.

  • Best Original Research: "Frailty Trajectories and Mortality Risk" by Dr. A. Johnson et al.
  • Best Review: "Inflammaging and Age-Related Diseases" by Prof. C. Ferrucci et al.
  • Best Early Career Paper: "Social Isolation and Cognitive Decline" by Dr. K. Yamamoto
